When you ask Claude, Gemini, or ChatGPT to build you a tool, the preview is trapped inside your chat window - refresh the page and everything you typed disappears, and nobody else can open it. **VibeBackr turns your AI creations into real websites with their own live link, Google sign-in, and cloud memory that stays saved.**

## How It Works: Pick Your AI & Start in 60 Seconds {#how-it-works}

You don't need to write code, buy a domain, or set up a server. Choose the AI assistant you use and follow the 3 steps:

:::tabs Choose your AI assistant
### Claude
:::flow
### Add VibeBackr to Claude
On **Claude Desktop**, download the 1-click extension and open it. Or in **Claude Settings > Connectors**, add a custom connector with our URL and sign in once with Google:

:::cta
- [Download for Claude (1-Click)](/vibebackr.mcpb){.btn-primary .sm}
- `https://vibebac.kr/mcp`
:::

### Ask Claude to Build Your App
Describe the tool you want in plain English - *"Build a shared family recipe book"* or *"Make a client request tracker my team can sign into."*

### Open Your Live Link & Share It
Claude builds your app and publishes it straight to its own web address (like `your-app.vibebac.kr`). Open it on your phone or send it to anyone.
:::

### Gemini
:::flow
### Paste the URL in Customizations
Open the **Customise > Connectors > + Custom** popup, choose to connect an MCP server, paste this URL, click next, follow onscreen prompts to sign in once with your Google account:

:::cta
- `https://vibebac.kr/mcp`
:::

### Ask Gemini to Build Your App
Tell Gemini what you want to make in plain English - *"Build a group trip planner where friends can sign in with Google and vote on hotels."*

### Open Your Live Link & Share It
Gemini sets up your cloud memory and publishes your finished app straight to its own live web link (`your-app.vibebac.kr`).
:::

### ChatGPT
:::flow
### Connect in ChatGPT Settings
Open **Settings > Connectors > Create Custom Connector** in ChatGPT, paste this URL, and sign in once with your Google account:

:::cta
- `https://vibebac.kr/mcp`
:::

### Ask ChatGPT to Build Your App
Enable the VibeBackr connector in your chat and describe the tool you want in plain English - zero coding required.

### Open Your Live Link & Share It
ChatGPT creates your cloud storage and publishes your app to its own live web address (`your-app.vibebac.kr`) ready to share with anyone.
:::

### Cursor & Copilot
:::flow
### Add the MCP Server URL
In Cursor, Windsurf, or VS Code (GitHub Copilot), open **MCP Settings > Add Server**, paste this URL, and authorize once in your browser:

:::cta
- `https://vibebac.kr/mcp`
:::

### Ask Your Agent to Build & Deploy
Ask your agent to build a tool with Google login and saved cloud data. It automatically provisions the database and wires up sign-in.

### Instant Live Hosting
Your agent deploys your app straight to `your-app.vibebac.kr` - or connects your local React/Next.js frontend in one line.
:::

### Other AI (Copy Prompt)
Using a regular web chat without custom connectors? Just copy and paste this starter prompt into **any** AI chat alongside your idea:

:::prompt Copy & Paste Into Any AI Chat
Use VibeBackr (https://vibebac.kr/llms.txt) to build, store, and host my app so it saves data in the cloud and has Google sign-in.
- If you support MCP: connect to https://vibebac.kr/mcp, create a collection (turn on auto_invite_shares if people can share items), and publish the finished app with publish_app so I get a live shareable link.
- If you don't support MCP: give me the collection setup to paste into https://vibebac.kr/login, then build the single-page app using <script src="https://vibebac.kr/vibebackr.js"></script> with Google sign-in (vibe.loginWithGoogle()).

## What VibeBackr Gives Your AI Apps {#what-is-it}

Normally, AI previews are just temporary drawings on your screen. VibeBackr gives your AI the missing pieces so your app actually works in the real world:

:::features
### A Real Web Link You Can Share
Every app gets its own live web address (`your-app.vibebac.kr`) that opens on any phone, tablet, or laptop - ready to bookmark or drop into a group chat.

### Cloud Memory That Never Disappears
Whatever you or your guests type, add, or check off is saved safely in the cloud. Close the tab, come back next week, or switch devices and everything is still there.

### Google Sign-In & Automatic Invites
Keep your app **private** just for you, **share** specific items with someone's email address (they'll be invited automatically!), or make it **public** for anyone with the link.
:::
